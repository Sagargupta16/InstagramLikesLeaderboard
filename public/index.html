Instagram Likes Leaderboard

Analyze liker identities returned for your recent posts and compare them with accounts you follow.

How to use

  1. Use a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) with developer tools; phone browsers have no console to paste into
  2. Log in to instagram.com and open your own profile page (instagram.com/your_username/)
  3. Click the "Copy Code" button below
  4. Open Developer Tools (F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Option+I)
  5. Go to the Console tab
  6. Paste the code and press Enter
  7. Click Start scan; compatible saved results may be reused automatically, otherwise leave this tab open until the scan completes

Try the demo Install userscript

The demo runs a full scan on generated sample data, with no login and no requests, and works on phones. The userscript needs a manager such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey; it adds a Likes Leaderboard button on instagram.com in place of the console steps.

The scan runs in this tab and saves completed results in this browser only. This page loads no analytics or external fonts. Fixed conservative pacing reduces request pressure but cannot guarantee avoiding Instagram throttling, checkpoints, temporary restrictions, or enforcement.
